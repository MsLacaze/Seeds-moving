15-minute science mission

Mission 1: Choose the escape kit

A seed needs to get away from its parent plant, where it will face less competition and may colonise a new place. Choose the feature that gives each seed its best chance.

Mission 2: Seed detective

Match each seed feature to its most likely way of travelling. Think about what the structure actually does.

Mission 3: The survival argument

A tawa tree’s fruit is eaten by a kererū. The seed survives the journey and lands far from the parent tree. Write a 5–7 sentence scientific explanation of how this helps the seedling. Use at least three of these words: competition, dispersal, adaptation, colonise, reserves. Include one possible disadvantage of this strategy.
